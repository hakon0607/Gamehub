## 2. What GameHub is

GameHub is a free program for Windows 10 and 11. It finds the games already
installed on your PC, shows them in one library, starts them through the
launcher they belong to, and adds tools around them: instant replay,
screenshots, a freeze function, playtime statistics, quests and wallpapers.

GameHub runs on your own PC. Your library, your clips, your screenshots and
your settings stay on your machine. Nothing about your games is uploaded, and
there is no account to create.

## 6. Freezing games, replay and anti-cheat — read this one

**"Freeze the game" (F7) suspends the game's processes.** That is exactly the
kind of interference that anti-cheat systems such as BattlEye and Easy
Anti-Cheat are built to detect, and most online games forbid it in their own
terms of use. Using it in an online game may get your game account suspended
or permanently banned by that game's publisher.

The same caution applies to any use of GameHub in a competitive online game.

**You use these features at your own risk.** The author cannot lift a ban, has
no relationship with the games companies, and is not liable for the loss of a
game account, its contents or anything you paid for it. If you are in any
doubt, use Freeze only in single-player games that are not protected by
anti-cheat.

## 7. Recording, screenshots and the clipboard

Instant replay records **your whole screen**, not just the game. Anything
visible while it runs can end up in a clip: a video call, a message window, a
password manager, someone else's face. Screenshots work the same way, and the
clipboard history stores what you copy.

All of it is written to your own disk and never uploaded by GameHub. But you
are responsible for what you record and what you do with it afterwards.
Recording other people may require their agreement where you live, and
publishing a clip from a game is governed by that game's own rules.
